The two methods are sampled over the same alpha window and run through the same estimator, so the only difference between these numbers is the aerodynamics. They are not expected to agree in LEVEL — this sweep is inviscid and carries no viscous Cm — so the row that matters is the middle one. LL reporting a sign change that the VLM does not see places the nonlinearity in LL's viscous Cm or its discretization, and is not by itself a verdict on the aeroplane (FINDINGS §21).

One mechanism, not two: the station where the local closure angle first crosses the threshold defines an enlarged effective base, and unrecovered base pressure is charged there. The coefficient (CD_BASE) is declared and uncalibrated — matched to AeroSandbox's own low-Mach base-drag value so a future numeric cross-check compares like with like, not fitted to anything this project has measured. Angles use the d_eq-equivalent axisymmetric convention, so a 68 × 88 section's vertical-plane closure is ~14% steeper in tangent than reported here.

Notes

  • Stall via wing-level CLmax knockdown; critical-section method arrives at M2/M3.
  • Smooth polars only; tripped-polar dual evaluation arrives at M2.
  • Propulsion chain is uncalibrated (no measurement path) — rankings over absolutes.
  • THE HEADLINE NUMBER IS SET BY AN AIRWORTHINESS FILTER, NOT BY THE AEROPLANE'S BEST POINT: the sweep peaks at 122.62 min at 9.50 m/s, and that point is excluded by static_margin_min. What is reported is 121.15 min at 10.00 m/s — 1.47 min less (static_margin_min dropped 1 of 15 trimmed points at or above v_min). Read the reported trim deflection, advance ratio and CL as properties of the ONE point that survived the filters, not as evidence the design is comfortably inside them.
  • NOT PROVEN INFEASIBLE — trim did not converge at V = 8.0 m/s (the 2-D root-find ran out of progress from every start it was given). These points are excluded from the sweep because there is no operating point to report, not because none exists; read them as unknown rather than as a limit of the aeroplane.
  • The static margin's sign change SURVIVES a 16-panel mesh (0.0890 → 0.0843), so refinement does not explain it. Combined with an inviscid VLM sweep that is monotone over the same window, the nonlinearity is the VISCOUS Cm at this Reynolds number — treat it as a property of the aeroplane.
  • CHAMPION STATIC MARGIN IS MESH-DEPENDENT: 0.0890 at the in-loop resolution against 0.0843 at 16 panels/section. The stability window is 0.07 wide and this moves the answer inside it.
  • THE FINENESS CEILING IS BINDING: the pod sits at f = 8.000000 against a declared ceiling of 8. That ceiling is a MODEL-validity bound — the Hoerner form factor keeps falling to f ~ 16 while the real minimum-drag band for a body of revolution is f ~ 6-7 — so the optimizer is being stopped by a declared number rather than by the aeroplane, and the slenderness it wants is not established to be real. Landing on it is a defect report against the fuselage drag model, not an optimum.
  • THE BOAT-TAIL FLOOR IS STILL ACTIVE: pod_tail sits at 1.800000 x d_eq against a floor of 1.8, with theta_max 18.9 deg against a 12 deg separation threshold. The floor is a PROXY for the afterbody physics and was kept for one battery to find out whether the new base-drag term had made it inactive. It has not: the term does not hold the tail cone open on its own, so the floor must NOT be deleted as a measured no-op and the correlation constants are what to revisit (docs/FUSELAGE_DRAG_PLAN.md 7).
  • M3 NLP: trimmed (explicit deflection), SM window, gust margin, spar stress/deflection sizing, ballast cap, battery-position balance.
  • Adaptive multistart: nominal and perturbed_0 converged to strict numerical consensus, so perturbed_1 was skipped as redundant. Exact tolerances and observed errors are recorded under performance.optimization.multistart_consensus.
  • RESUMED, not re-solved: multistart, priced equipment_fit, study fuselage_topology, study prop_choice, study tail_type, study wing_dihedral_form, winglet study came entirely from checkpoints written by an earlier run of the same model (425e66793194). The physics matches — that is what the fingerprint guarantees — but this run did not re-search those phases, so their 0.0-minute entries above are a resume, not a skip.
